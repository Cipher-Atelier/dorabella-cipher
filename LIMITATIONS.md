# Limitations

- No accepted decipherment, plaintext, key or new letter assignment is established.
- The source inventory is accounted for, but this public deposit intentionally excludes prohibited and unresolved material. It is not a raw dump of every original archive or conversation.
- Source ZIPs are retained intact outside Git, with exact versions and byte hashes. Whole ZIP publication would reintroduce third-party/source/private material and is not permitted.
- Raw source pages/images/books, British Library reference-only images and every derivative, personal correspondence, private locators, operational wrappers, dependencies, scoring/dictionary/pattern assets and binary files needing review are omitted or held externally.
- Some own reports contain private locators and remain external unchanged. They are not silently sanitized; English summaries here are separate documents.
- Removing model/scorer assets and some source/control material means the curated deposit is not a standalone numerical reproduction environment. Original checksum files may name deliberately omitted assets or earlier layouts.
- The full round-13 Library package was used, not the sparse local benchmark folder. That original compact bundle itself omits large numeric checkpoint arrays; their recorded hashes are not their bytes.
- R6 external v4 is 52/52 completed calibration, not the full project archive. Its stale embedded labels remain preserved rather than retrospectively corrected.
- Old regression controls and nulls were reused; real-note key selection used all 87 signs. Adaptive selection, related transcriptions and separately generated nulls limit inferential claims. Descriptive tails are not solution probabilities or globally calibrated significance.
- BE-W16 v2 is incomplete/NO_GO. One failed exact recovery, one incomplete case and six unstarted cases do not constitute eight empirical failures or a measured general sensitivity.
- Byte hashes establish equality to observed copies, not historical authenticity, authorship, rights, scientific correctness or completeness of an original run.
- Static privacy/rights screening is conservative and heuristic. It does not prove universal absence of private material or settle third-party redistribution rights; identified uncertain objects are withheld.
- Primary witnesses and external bibliographic pages were not newly investigated during archive assembly. Historical AI-assisted checks and reported reruns were not repeated. No independent human decipherment validation is claimed.
