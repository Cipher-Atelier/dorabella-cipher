# Rights and attribution

No blanket repository license has been applied. Authorship and existing source-specific terms remain with their respective creators and rights holders. This repository does not grant rights to papers, books, transcriptions, images, models, dictionaries, pattern databases or control corpora owned by others.

The public selection contains screened own-research code, reports, saved results, logs and curated metadata. Cited sources remain credited. Raw third-party source books/pages/images, source scans/crops, unclear model and dictionary assets, upstream modules and restricted materials are held or excluded. A file's availability in a private account does not clear its redistribution.

The British Library reference-only image and all derivatives, personal correspondence, credentials and raw private chats are prohibited from transfer. The broader private source inventory is not public. Original reports held for privacy/rights reasons retain their original bytes externally; no silent redaction or false licensing has occurred.
