## Storage and interpretation

The public capsule includes selected `.py` and `.md` originals only. It excludes source/dependency/cache directories, numeric models and result arrays, private locators and unselected source notes. Static screening found no credential, signed-URL, email or private-chat-locator patterns in the final selection. This is conservative triage, not a blanket rights determination.

The complete local tree is preserved separately in a private compressed archive. The public capsule cannot resolve every original relative link or reproduce every experiment by itself. Final reports' historical verification claims were read, not rerun; the closeout performed only file, path, hash and archive checks.
