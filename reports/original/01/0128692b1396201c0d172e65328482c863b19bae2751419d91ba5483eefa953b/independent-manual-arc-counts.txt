Uncertainty and revision record
Global 41: Initial whole-row inspection provisionally favoured 3, alternative 2.
An isolated crop changed the preferred reading to 2, alternative 3, before receipt of information about the earlier narrow inspection.
The initial apparent third lobe is the central thick junction. The isolated crop supports an upper right-facing semicircle plus a lower oblique cup; the junction does not resolve into a separate semicircle.
Precise original-pixel crop box: x=149..165, y=42..61; PIL box (149,42,166,62).
The 2/3 ambiguity is retained rather than silently replacing the provisional result.

Consecutive-three conclusion (assessed only after all 87 counts)
No three consecutive 3-arc glyphs occur, either within rows or across row boundaries.
