Liszt fragment source audit — 2026-10-20

Outcome: Not an established parallel known-plaintext pair. The linked image supports a conventional count of 18 curved glyph groups, with a separate terminal dash. The quoted phrase GETS YOU TO JOY, AND HYSTERIOUS has 25 alphabetic letters (4+3+2+3+3+10). These lengths are incompatible under one glyph = one letter. This rejects that specific pairing under that assumption, not the historical phrase under every possible encoding.
