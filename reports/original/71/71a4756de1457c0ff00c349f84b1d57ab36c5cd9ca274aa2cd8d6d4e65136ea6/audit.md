# Dorabella: bounded toposcope source audit

Date: 2026-10-20 UTC.

## Result

The provided source set does not supply an explicit 24-entry or eight-directions-by-three-letters cipher lookup. The visible material shows a geographical direction finder: a central compass, named landmarks and map features, a distance scale, a map note, and a separate circumferential inscription. None of the inspected images visibly labels three alphabet letters for each of eight cipher directions or specifies how one, two, and three arcs select them.

This is a source-specification result, not a proof that no conceivable association exists. The proposed relation remains underdetermined unless additional historical evidence supplies a selection and ordering rule. No initials, bearing bins, landmark selection, date arithmetic, or new candidate alphabet was introduced or tested.

## Page evidence

Daniel Hartmeier, “Clues (or red herrings) to the Dorabella Cipher”:
https://www.benzedrine.ch/dorabella.html

The relevant discussion is under “Speculation.” It proposes that the monument's engravings might associate three letters with eight directions. The page does not identify the selected letters, the start position or direction of reading, a rule for grouping them, or the ordering of each triplet. Earlier on the page is a separate illustrative alphabet explicitly described as arbitrary; that is not a transcription of a monument legend. The update supplies four image links, without converting the proposal into a defined key.

## Image evidence

1. https://www.benzedrine.ch/dorabella-topodisk.gif

500 × 500 pixels. A complete circular map drawing/reproduction with a compass at the centre; cardinal direction labels; numerous geographical names both within the map and around the outer annulus; scale at upper right; a boxed note in the lower-right part. The outer labels are geographical entries distributed around the horizon, not visibly eight groups of three single letters. The small text is not fully readable at this resolution. The boxed note cannot be fully transcribed reliably from this file.

2. https://www.benzedrine.ch/dorabella-toposcopering.jpg

326 × 318 pixels. This is a schematic drawing of the ring inscription, not a photograph. It shows the continuous sentence “THE EARTH IS THE LORDS AND THE FULNESS THEREOF”, with dots separating words, plus a separate north arrow inside. It does not draw eight sectors, mark triplets, label symbol arc counts, or give any extraction instruction. The north arrow establishes an orientation for the drawing; by itself it does not select or order 24 letters.

3. https://www.benzedrine.ch/dorabella-toposcope.jpg

2560 × 1920 pixels. Oblique photograph of the complete monument top. The same geographical disk is visible under glass. The compass is a map-direction graphic; the place names, scale, and map note are geographical information, not a visible substitution legend. Condensation/reflection obscures portions of the annulus and small text. A small plate reads “REPLACED FOLLOWING THEFT 2000”. This is direct evidence of what the photographed object says; it does not establish the wording or exact design present in 1897.

4. https://www.benzedrine.ch/dorabella-toposcope2.jpg

2560 × 1920 pixels. Opposite oblique view. It shows the outer stone inscription more clearly in places, the same map/compass structure, the boxed map note, and the replacement plate. Broad glare affects the upper and left map/rim areas, and the note's fine print is not completely secure. No explicit eight-by-three alphabet is visible in the readable areas.

## What remains missing

To claim an unambiguous key from this material, the missing evidence is an explicit or independently justified instruction selecting 24 particular letters and assigning each to a direction and arc count. Merely resolving more landmark names would not, by itself, provide that instruction. To claim categorically that no relevant small inscription appears anywhere on the object, one would additionally need readable views of all currently obscured annular labels and the complete fine print of the map note. The supplied pictures also do not establish identity with the 1897 state.

## Scope and stopping condition

Only the supplied page and its four linked images were inspected. The actual links, not guessed filenames, established the image URLs. The first two images were viewed in the cloud browser and the photographs from local downloads. No external outreach, paid work, new key search, solver/scorer, or reopening of prior geometric searches occurred. The bounded image set has been exhausted; this check stops here.
