Original source files are included unchanged as read-only evidence with their LICENCE (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike4.0). See https://github.com/bmhauer/hhk-decipherment/tree/2c7ad49cd4f4fa852168a8ce295bf739bf076adc .

The new analyze.py is a Python stdlib two-string mathematical diagnostic, not the Perl solver. Changes include sparse sequential table extraction, exact rational arithmetic, no-space segmentation expressed in Python, finite receipts and limits; it has no search/neighbor/key mechanism. It is offered under the same CC BY-SA4.0 terms insofar as it adapts the original algorithms. Neither authorship endorsement nor Perl numerical equivalence is asserted. The full external model tables are not duplicated in this archive.
